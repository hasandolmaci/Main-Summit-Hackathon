**Implement first:** trademarks, industry experience, prior founding/outcomes, scientific output, founder count. They add independent evidence beyond website richness; count is nearly free once founder identities are resolved. Academic enrichment should first target deep-tech leads.

**Down-weight:** pricing, waitlists, Impressum, TLS age, raw logos and jobs-page presence have no strong standalone validation located here. Remove “missing = zero” and the 70% coverage exclusion; report uncertainty separately. Team complementarity is modest evidence, not grounds for a large bonus.

**Additional sources and exclusions:**

- [Lyonnet & Stern, 2025 working paper](https://www.iese.edu/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/6.-paper-Stern-et-al.pdf): 121,936 French firms; VCs overweight elite education relative to performance. No convincing elite-employer uplift verified.
- [Bracht, Mahieu & Vanhaverbeke, 2024, JBV](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jbusvent.2024.106380): German IAB/ZEW evidence links UG to less **debt**, not first VC; do not transplant Delaware effects.
- [Te et al., 2023, JFDS](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfds.2023.100099): Crunchbase/LinkedIn model AUC **.84**, founder-count importance; later enrichment and already-seeded population limit applicability.
- [RIETI web-traffic study](https://www.rieti.go.jp/jp/publications/dp/24e021.pdf) uses SEMrush histories: fails your free-data constraint. Domain spelling/TLD effects remain unverified.
