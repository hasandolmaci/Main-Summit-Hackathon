Task: write scripts/label_funding.py that, for every row of an input cohort CSV (argument 1, e.g. raw/cohort_utum.csv), decides whether the company raised its FIRST INSTITUTIONAL funding (VC, CVC, angel fund, equity-taking accelerator like YC/Antler/Techstars, priced round, SAFE/convertible from an institution) within 18 months after asof_date, and writes raw/labels_<input-stem>.csv with columns: name,website,asof_date,y_label,y_round_date,y_round_type,y_amount_eur,y_dead,funding_status,evidence_url,evidence_quote,checked_sources,needs_review.
Method (free sources only):
(a) Google News RSS: https://news.google.com/rss/search?q=QUERY&hl=de&gl=DE&ceid=DE:de and also hl=en-US&gl=US&ceid=US:en, with QUERY = "NAME" (Finanzierung OR Finanzierungsrunde OR Seed OR Pre-Seed OR investiert OR raises OR funding). Parse pubDate. Keep only items whose title contains the company name and a funding word. Earliest such date = candidate round date.
(b) deutsche-startups.de search: https://www.deutsche-startups.de/?s=NAME (parse result titles and dates).
(c) Fetch the company homepage (today) and look for 'backed by', 'investors', 'funded by', and investor names (HTGF, High-Tech Gruenderfonds, Y Combinator, Antler, Techstars, Earlybird, Cherry Ventures, Project A, Speedinvest, La Famiglia, Point Nine, UVC Partners, Atlantic Labs, Picus, 468 Capital, Vorwerk Ventures, BayernKapital, Seedcamp, Entrepreneur First, First Momentum, Plug and Play, Redstone, Visionaries Club, xdeck, Possible Ventures, Amino Collective, IBB Ventures, eCAPITAL, Matterwave, Bayern Kapital). That is evidence of funding at an unknown date.
(d) Dead: website unreachable or parked, or news with insolvent/closed/shut down.
Decision: y_label=1 if the earliest institutional round date is within (asof_date, asof_date + 18 months]. y_label=0 if all checks ran and no funding evidence was found, OR the first round came later than 18 months after asof_date. If funding exists but its date is unknown or before asof_date: y_label empty and funding_status=UNKNOWN or FUNDED_BEFORE. Grants (EXIST, prizes, competitions) are NOT funding. Rows with asof_date after 2025-05-20: y_label empty, funding_status=IMMATURE. needs_review=1 for ambiguous cases (common-word company names, only one weak hit, date conflicts).
Run it on raw/cohort_utum.csv and report: counts of y_label 1/0/empty, and 10 example positives with evidence. Only write scripts/label_funding.py and raw/labels_*.csv (plus your cache folder).
