# Decision brief: a logistic-regression benchmark for scoring NEW startups

Context: HTGF hackathon. Read DATA_README.md, data_dictionary.csv, prior_weights.json, backtest.csv (205 rows, 107 cols; y_label: 13 positives, 77 negatives; y_label_incl_dead: 13 / 103), prompts/review_astra.md and prompts/review_opus.md (earlier reviews: learned weights unstable, LOO AUC ~0.5-0.6 with 14 positives; pricing and Impressum strongest; strong year confounding).
The user's metric framework: Eligibility gates (pass/fail: not funded, German, alive, independent, commercial, sector, not noise, age<=3y, active, not faked), Startup Quality 0-100 (founder industry experience, complementary team, prior exit, execution continuity, technical substance, patents, product readiness, experimentation tools, momentum, customers, outside OSS, online attention, launch spread, buyer/problem, market health, regulated/hardware, deep tech, B2B, uni spin-off, programme validation, similarity), Investment Opportunity 0-100 (raise-soon, venture age, head start, stealth founder, hackathon, investor attention, hiring mix, own domain, Impressum, legal entity, pricing/pilot, open roles, thesis match). The user explicitly wants LOGISTIC REGRESSION to make weights / a real working benchmark for new startups.

Decide (be concrete and honest given n=13 positives):
1. Model design: which 5-8 features to put in the logistic regression (only point-in-time, non-leaky, decently filled columns), how to encode missing values (indicator vs category), year-band control, regularisation (L2, strong), and how to combine with the frozen prior weights: e.g. Bayesian/ridge shrinkage TOWARD the prior weights (offset or prior-mean penalty), or blend w = lambda*learned + (1-lambda)*prior with lambda chosen by LOO; or Firth logistic regression. Recommend one.
2. Validation that is honest with 13 positives: repeated stratified CV or LOO, within-year comparison, compare vs prior-only score and vs constant; bootstrap CI for AUC/Brier; coefficient stability (sign consistency across bootstrap).
3. Turning it into a benchmark for new startups: score 0-100 (probability-calibrated? percentile vs the backtest population?), how eligibility gates apply first, how Quality and Opportunity sub-scores map from the LR + prior weights, how to handle features that new startups have but the backtest lacks (e.g. founder data, employee count today) — use prior weights for those.
4. Exact deliverables: scripts/train_benchmark.py (python, sklearn available) producing models/benchmark_weights.json, reports/benchmark_report.md (validation numbers, coefficient table with CIs, stability), and scripts/score_startups.py that scores any CSV with the same columns (e.g. discovery_leads.csv / new startups) into quality, opportunity and total 0-100 with an explanation of top contributing signals.
5. What must be said in the pitch about this model's limits.

Output (under ~650 words): recommended design, validation plan, benchmark mapping, file/function spec, pitch caveats, confidence. Do not modify files.
