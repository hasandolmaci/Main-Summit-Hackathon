Gates: No institutional investor yet (founders' money, F&F, grants/prizes any amount, business angels up to ~EUR150k pass; unknown angel size -> review; any VC fund, equity-taking accelerator, priced/announced round, or >EUR150k angels -> excluded); Not in HTGF portfolio; German seat or operating base; Alive (not closed, insolvent or acquired); Independent (not corporate-owned); Commercial (not e.V., gGmbH, student club or pure research); Investable sector (not consulting, agency, retail, real estate, gambling or crypto token); Not noise (not hobby, student assignment or company-less open source); Age <= 3 years; Active in the last 90 days; Not faked (no star bursts, new-account stars or bought upvotes); More than 25 people (review flag).

Startup quality 0-100: Founder worked in this industry before; Relevant skills over credentials; Complementary team (technical + commercial); Prior successful founder / exit; Execution continuity (weeks with commits in the last 8); Founder background; Founder building (actively shipping); Founder commitment (full-time, named in the Impressum); Technical substance (working artifact, benchmark, paper); Patents; Product readiness (live app, docs, first TLS certificate, waitlist); Experimentation tools (A/B testing, analytics stack); Momentum vs own baseline with time decay; Verified customers / pilots; Outside open-source contributions; Online attention / web mentions; Launch-platform spread; Clear paying buyer and costly problem; Market health; Regulated or hardware market; University spin-off origin; Programme validation (EXIST, SPRIND, incubator, competition); Similarity to past winners (embeddings).
3. Investment opportunity 0-100: Raise-soon score (GmbH i.G., pricing live, founding-engineer job, pitching at events); Venture age (best at 3-18 months); Head start in days vs databases/press; Stealth-founder signal; Hackathon-to-company signal; Investor attention; Hiring mix (engineering vs sales hires); Own domain; Impressum with named founders; Legal entity; Pricing / pilot offer; Open roles; Thesis match (AI / deep tech / industrial, repeatable product); No contact or founder name (penalty); No legal entity yet (penalty).
How we score: peer percentile within sector and age group; missing data = 0; confidence/coverage % (>= 70% to be ranked). Eligibility is pass/fail; only startups that pass get scored. Quality is the main 0-100 score. Opportunity decides the order among good startups (who to call this week).
